How to play · วิธีเล่น

1Cook & read. Cook a dish in the mini kitchen, then open its Story to read all 3 layers. +1 KP (knowledge point).

2Answer the quick question. A right answer gives +1 shell and +3 KP.

3Feed your sapling. Tap shells in the bottom corner. Grow it, fill your field guide, and spend KP to unlock hats and friends.

4Earn points. Every action scores. Finish the daily quests and climb today’s ranking. Each dish can be cooked once a day, so come back tomorrow!
